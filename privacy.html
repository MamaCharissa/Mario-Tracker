Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. About Mario Tracker

Mario Tracker is a Discord bot that records and tracks Warp Rush activity in supported Mario gaming communities. It provides player statistics, leaderboards, milestones, reward tracking, leaderboard imports and tools for managing tracking records.

This policy explains what information Mario Tracker processes, why it is used, how it is stored and how users can contact the bot owner about their data.

2. Information we collect and store

Depending on how the bot is used, Mario Tracker may store information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Discord identifiers: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs and message IDs.
  • Player information: Discord display names, game-related player names and associated Warp Rush totals.
  • Game activity records: the date and time an event was recorded, relevant message references and associated player information.
  • Leaderboard imports: imported player names, totals, message references and import records.
  • Milestone and reward records: milestones reached, reward details, issuance status and relevant administrative records.
  • Blacklist records: Discord user IDs, display names, exclusion reasons and entry dates.
  • Server configuration: configured tracking and audit channels, milestone settings and approved-server information.
  • Audit records: records of relevant tracking events, imports and reward actions.

Mario Tracker is not designed to collect passwords, payment card details, home addresses or government identification documents.

3. How information is used

Information is used to detect Warp Rush announcements in configured channels, process relevant message content and embeds (including edited announcements), associate activity with players, calculate statistics and leaderboards, maintain imports and rewards, apply blacklist rules, keep relevant audit records and configure the bot for approved servers.

4. Messages and message content

Mario Tracker processes messages and embeds from the designated game bot in a server-configured tracking channel. Relevant game-related information is used to identify events and update player statistics. The database may store event details and message references; the bot is not intended to archive every message sent by server members or monitor unrelated conversations.

5. Discord identifiers and player names

Discord user IDs and player names help distinguish players and associate activity with the correct account. If an event cannot be matched to a Discord account, a game-related player name may be retained to preserve the tracking record.

6. Who can access information?

Records are associated with the relevant Discord server. Users may access their own statistics and progress through member-facing commands. Server administrators with the required permissions may use administrative features such as configuration, imports, audit tools and reward management. Certain bot-owner functions are restricted to the bot owner. Player names and totals may appear in leaderboards or other tracking outputs intended for the community.

7. Storage and security

Mario Tracker stores operational records in a database on its hosting service. Database backups may also be created as part of the bot's startup process. Access to the hosting account, database, bot credentials and backups should be restricted to authorised individuals. No internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and deletion requests

Tracking records are retained for as long as they are needed to provide the bot's features, preserve player progress and support legitimate server administration. Some records may remain in backups after removal from the active database. Backup retention depends on the hosting and backup-management arrangements.

To ask about information associated with your Discord account, request correction of a record or request deletion where appropriate, contact the bot owner using the details below. Requests will be considered in light of the purpose of the records, applicable legal requirements and technical ability to remove the information, including from backups where appropriate.

9. Third-party services

Mario Tracker relies on Discord to provide bot functionality. Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The bot is also hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may process information as necessary to operate the service and store its database. Please consult those providers' published policies for details of their independent practices.

10. Children's privacy

Mario Tracker is a gaming-community tool, not a service designed to collect personal information directly from children. Server administrators should ensure their use of the bot is appropriate for their community and complies with applicable rules and laws.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Mario Tracker's features, data practices, hosting arrangements or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published at the public privacy-policy URL listed in the Discord Developer Portal.
